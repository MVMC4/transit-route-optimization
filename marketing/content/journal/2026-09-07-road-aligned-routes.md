## The problem

A straight line between stops is easy to draw and wrong for a rider. It hides turns, makes nearby routes hard to compare, and can imply that a combi crosses places where no road exists.

## The approach

Tsela requests road-following geometry and keeps the original stop order. Community contributors can add steering points when the first preview chooses the wrong turn.

- Stops remain meaningful boarding or alighting places.
- Steering points describe the road without pretending to be stops.
- A route stays unpublished until it has been reviewed.

## The next check

Road alignment is necessary, but not sufficient. Local riders still need to confirm whether each corridor matches the path a combi actually takes.

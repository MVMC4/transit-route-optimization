## What changed

The rider application is no longer a collection of features competing on one screen. Home, trip planning, route exploration, community work, and the field guide each have a clear place.

- Saved routes return on the home screen.
- Recently viewed corridors are kept on the device.
- The navigation dock stays at the bottom on desktop and mobile.
- The field guide now uses the same rounded, bold visual language as the rest of the rider app.

## Why it matters

A transit tool should feel familiar before a rider has learned it. The home screen answers the first three questions quickly: where can I go, what have I saved, and what changed recently?

## Still to validate

The wording, route names, operating directions, and travel guidance still need review by people who use these corridors every day.

# Research notes: Botswana Public Transport blog export

**Source supplied by project owner:** `combi-blog-export.pdf`, titled “Botswana Public Transport – YourBotswana,” six printed pages, exported 2026-09-07. The article UI says it was posted roughly ten years earlier.

## Product-useful observations

- Combis may leave a rank when full rather than on a published timetable.
- Riders can ask the driver/conductor to stop or to call the next stop.
- Leave a buffer when arrival time matters.
- Carry small denominations to reduce payment friction.
- Arrive early for long-distance services because seats may fill.
- Bring water and basic provisions for longer trips.

## Deliberately not treated as current fact

The fare values, exact departure time, route-specific behavior, wait-time anecdotes, and broad judgments about safety or conduct are historical and subjective. The Rider Guide paraphrases durable practical advice and tells users to verify mutable details locally.

## Design implications

- Live-trip mode needs a user-selected alighting stop and an early warning.
- Route pages need direction and ordered stops, not only a line.
- Community reports should eventually carry observation dates and moderation status.
- The planner must describe estimates honestly and must not present itself as a live timetable.

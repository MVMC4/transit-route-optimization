# Marketing product-proof review

Reviewed 19 September 2026 using official product sites.

- Transit leads with one plain-language outcome and follows it with app imagery and a step-by-step story: nearby transit, planning, GO guidance, and community.
- Citymapper is even more restrained: one proposition, real phone proof, and limited competing navigation.
- Mapbox separates platform categories such as maps, navigation, search, and data, while reserving heavier interactive demonstrations for product/developer contexts.

Tsela therefore uses a server-rendered SVG route preview on marketing: origin, road-following route, direction, destination, and one recommendation card. The live MapLibre map stays in the rider app where search and interaction justify its JavaScript, tile traffic, and rendering cost. The homepage keeps one dominant rider action and progressive doorways to services, developer access, and the build journal.

References: Transit (`https://transitapp.com/`), Citymapper (`https://citymapper.com/`), Mapbox (`https://www.mapbox.com/`), and Mapbox mapping concepts (`https://docs.mapbox.com/help/getting-started/maps/`).
